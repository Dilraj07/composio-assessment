The findings

Every claim below is a count off the enum-constrained records, not an assertion.

The blocker is almost never authentication.

0 apps hand out credentials in seconds and still refuse production traffic until a separate approval clears — developer tokens, app review, partner admission. Auth is a solved problem; commercial gating is not. Any toolkit roadmap built on "which auth does it use" will mis-rank its own backlog.

Honest limitations

90 of 100 apps. The source brief was truncated mid-way through category 10 ("AI, Research and Media-native"). No apps were invented to reach 100.

Gold set is — apps, a stratified sample — one obvious and one obscure app per category, weighted toward likely traps. Accuracy figures carry the confidence interval of that sample size.

Mismatches are classified, not just counted. Some are agent errors; some are cases where the enum itself was too loose, or where the vendor changed after the gold set was written. Only agent_wrong counts against the pipeline, and the class is shown per row above.

Pipeline source, methodology and the full rubric breakdown are in the repo. Every field carries its own evidence URL and confidence; nulls are real nulls.
